2Logo

One logo is in use: a teal dome (arc) with a yellow three-point star cut through it, a registered-trademark symbol at the right edge of the dome, and the wordmark "Pamar Enterprises" in teal with "Inc." in yellow beneath it.

Registered trademark. Pamar has confirmed the logo is a registered mark (®) and stays in use as-is on the new site. Treat the supplied files below as the master artwork. Do not redesign, re-proportion, re-typeset, or recolor it; any vector version must be a faithful trace. Keep the ® symbol on every reproduction of the dome-and-star mark and the full lockup.

Backgrounds it sits on

On the live site the logo only ever appears on white: a white angled panel in the header, a white footer band, and a white browser tab. It is never placed on teal, yellow, dark, or photographic backgrounds. No reversed (white) version exists.

Clear space and minimum size

The site defines no clear space or minimum size. The header renders the logo at 377 px wide on desktop and about 230 px wide on a 390 px phone; the footer uses 273 px.
Clear space: keep a margin equal to the height of the star (about one quarter of the logo height) on all sides. Minimum size: 140 px wide on screen and 1 inch (25 mm) in print for the full lockup; below that, use the mark alone at no less than 24 px.
clear space = star height

Do and don't

On white, from the supplied master file, ® intact. Matches every use on the site.
Cropping or hiding the ® symbol. It is part of the registered mark.
JPEG on a colored background. The baked-in white box shows.
On brand yellow. The star and "Inc." vanish into the background.
Stretched or squashed. Always scale proportionally.
On dark backgrounds. The teal wordmark drops to about 3.6:1 contrast; no reversed version exists.
Recolored. Teal and yellow are the identity; do not tint or grayscale.
Vector status: a faithful vector trace is still needed unless Pamar has the original artwork. The supplied 684 px PNG covers the header at 2x but is too small for print, signage, or hero use, and it carries an opaque white background. Trace it exactly (a registered mark must not be redrawn) and set the fills to #0094A5 and #F7C92D. Also note a site bug: the og:image on every page points at logo-1.png, which is actually the MITA association logo, so social shares show the wrong logo.

Licensing

Open Sans and Montserrat are published under the SIL Open Font License 1.1. They may be self-hosted, embedded in the site, and used in print and PDF at no cost. Self-hosting (as the new site already does with Fontsource) removes the Google Fonts request and the related privacy consideration. Raleway is also OFL but does not need to be carried over.

The logo wordmark is not Open Sans or Montserrat. It is a bold, tight grotesque closest to Arial Bold or Helvetica Bold. Its source is unknown and it should be treated as artwork, not as a typeface to set text in.
Carry over Open Sans (300, 400, 700) as the single text family. Montserrat 400 is optional: it only ever appears as small uppercase UI labels, and Open Sans 700 uppercase with 1 px tracking reads the same at that size. Dropping it saves a font download.

5Imagery

The captured photos fall into three groups.

No photo direction, team portraits, equipment fleet shots, before-and-after project pairs, or drone footage beyond one aerial. The Careers page has no imagery at all.
Keep the documentary jobsite style; it is the brand's strongest asset and it is authentic. Commission or collect: a consistent set of 3:2 project photos per featured project, a few crew and leadership portraits, fleet and yard photos for the aggregate and topsoil sales pages, and one owned hero image to replace the stock sunset worker. Apply a consistent teal or near-black overlay for text-over-photo and keep the white frame treatment as an optional accent. Replace icon images with inline SVG so they scale and take the teal token.

6Voice and tone

Traits below are each backed by a line from the live copy.

Traits

Trait Evidence (quoted)
Plain and direct "Give us a call for pricing." · "Tell us about your project and we'll see how we can help."
Experience-led "Due to our many years of experience, we are often called upon to do some of the most complex specialty work."
Capable under pressure "We are the go-to resource for many Michigan municipalities when they need a quick turnaround."
Technical when it counts "Pipe Bursting 27,000LF of 6″ and 8″ Diameter pipe up to 10″ Diameter HDPE SDR pipe."
Safety as a value, not a slogan "seeking safety above & beyond MIOSHA standards" · "Weekly Crew Safety Meetings"
Family and community "a family owned business" · "Sabrina and I dropped off the donations to Caroline's House yesterday."
Occasionally over-claims "We're the best at delivering huge results" · "world class service and results" (both unsupported on the page)

Do and don't

Do
  • Lead with the job and the outcome: "Pump Station Installation with Wet Well, 4,200 LF Water Main."
  • Say who the work is for: cities, counties, MDOT, townships, private developers, and individuals with a truck.
  • Use first person plural: "we", "our team", "our clients".
  • Keep sentences short and concrete. Give quantities, materials, and locations.
  • End sections with a plain invitation: "Call us", "Tell us about your project".
Don't
  • Use superlatives without proof ("the best", "world class", "huge results").
  • Mix corporate abstraction with plain talk ("paving the way in technology and process advancements").
  • Use double exclamation marks or shouting titles ("Now Located On Pamar Court!!").
  • Leave typos in place: "it's new website", "half a decade", "everyday" (for "every day"), "clients success".
  • Write internal notes into public copy ("If you made a donation please let me know").

Naming conventions

The site uses four forms of the name, inconsistently:

Form Where it appears
Pamar Enterprises, Inc. History copy, "What We Do" paragraph (with comma)
Pamar Enterprises Inc. Logo, contact page H3, CTA button, news post titles (no comma)
Pamar Enterprises Page titles, copyright line, most body copy
Pamar Rarely, inside history copy ("Pamar began to specialize")
Use "Pamar Enterprises, Inc." (with comma) once per page where the legal entity matters (footer copyright, contact block, bid documents), "Pamar Enterprises" in running copy and titles, and "Pamar" only in conversational lines and internal headings. Confirm the punctuated legal name with the client (see open questions). Other terms to standardize: "water main" (two words, as the site writes it), "MDOT", "MIOSHA", "LF" for linear feet in project specs.
